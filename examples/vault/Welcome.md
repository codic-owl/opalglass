# A little space to think

Welcome to Opalglass. This is a folder of ordinary Markdown files, ready to become a home for your ideas.

## Start with a thought

Write in the source pane on the left. Read the native preview on the right. Save with Ctrl S, and your note stays right here on disk.

- Create a note with Ctrl N
- Find a note with Ctrl P
- Switch reading modes with Ctrl E
- Connect ideas with a wiki link

Follow [[Ideas/A garden of notes|A garden of notes]] to explore connected thinking, or open [[Keyboard shortcuts]] for a quick reference.

> Your notes belong to you. No account, no service, no special format.
